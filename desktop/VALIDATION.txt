Areion Flight Console and Python SDK 3.12.3
2026-11-19

Transmitter ON/OFF display
A compiled FC 3.2.0 publisher supplied serial DATA to the native desktop. The
test operated the Configuration → Input buttons and used a second window to
check profile reload. With the other sticks stationary, CH6 at 2000 µs showed
TX ON in green. The verified loss marker at 1000 µs changed TX, PPM, INPUT
and MODE to orange even while the FC continued receiving eight channels.
The pulse-stop method, signal recovery, AP/USB selection, flight-mode channel
conflict and refusal of indistinguishable ON/OFF frames were also exercised.
The verification workflow sent zero frames to the FC. Physical hardware was
not available for this test.
