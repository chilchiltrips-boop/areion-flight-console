The F450 tripod dynamics, rate controller, comparison experiments, assembly model and
synthesized motor sounds are adapted from chilchiltrips-boop/dronelab at
0101b6d77ed9750633baf991664f8fce274da8f6 (js/tripod-*.js and js/rate-pid.js).
The local controller uses the A1 stick scales, PID bounds, four-motor mixer,
throttle cutoff and output pulse limits from firmware/AreionBrowserAP. Mechanical
loads, motor speeds and thrust are model estimates. Geometry and Three.js come
from web/assembly. Practice runs locally. Optional receiver mirroring reads native Areion telemetry.
